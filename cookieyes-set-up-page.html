Let's Set Up Your Cookie Banner Together

A friendly, no-rush walkthrough for CookieYes — the little pop-up that keeps your website legally compliant. Follow along one picture at a time.

⏱ Takes about 15 minutes

What is a "cookie banner," and why does your website need one? Whenever a website quietly stores small bits of information in a visitor's browser (to remember their preferences, or to measure traffic), privacy laws like GDPR require you to ask permission first. CookieYes is the free-to-start tool that adds that polite little pop-up for you automatically.

This is a three-part journey. Part 1 creates your account and gets the banner live on your site. Part 2 is turning on the paid plan ($10/month) that this setup needs. Part 3 is just one invite so I can help manage things on the technical side.

Take your time — nothing here is permanent until you say so, and every screen has a "back" option if something looks off.

1

Creating Your Account & Banner

Go to app.cookieyes.com in your browser to begin.

1

Create your free account

This is just a normal sign-up — your email, your website's address, and a password you'll remember.

Fill in your email address, your website address, and choose a password. Tick the agreement box, then click Get Started.

✉️ Before you go any further: check your email inbox for a message from CookieYes and click the verification link inside it. Some steps ahead won't work properly until your email is confirmed — this only takes a moment.
2

Set up how your banner looks

This is just the appearance of your pop-up — you (or I) can always change the colors and wording later, so don't overthink this part.

Leave the language as English (unless you need another), pick a layout you like (Banner is a safe default), choose a colour, and click Next Step.

3

Choose how to install it

CookieYes needs to know how you'll connect it to your website. There are two options — one is simpler, and it's the one almost everyone should use.

Click Install manually on website. (Only choose the other option if you already use something called "Google Tag Manager" — most people don't.)

4

Copy your banner's code

This is a short piece of computer code that tells your website to show the banner. Don't worry about understanding it — you're just copying and handing it over.

Click Copy code. If you're doing this yourself, paste it right after the opening <head> tag in your site's code. If I'm handling the technical setup, you can simply let me know it's ready — I'll take it from here.

5

Verify it's working

This step just double-checks that the code landed in the right place on your site.

Click Verify. You should see a green checkmark confirming success.

6

Your banner is live! 🎉

Your dashboard will now show your banner as "Active." That means visitors to your site will start seeing the cookie pop-up right away.

✅ Part 1 complete — your cookie banner is up and running.
2

Turning On the $10/Month Plan

This step is required — without a paid plan, you won't be able to add me as a helper in Part 3.

7

Start the upgrade

On your dashboard, you'll notice a button mentioning a "Pro" trial. Don't let the name worry you — clicking it simply opens the full list of plans; you are not committing to Pro by clicking it.

Click the orange Try Pro for free button in the top-right corner.

8

Pick the "Basic" plan

You'll now see all four plans side by side. We only need the second one from the left.

Make sure Monthly is selected, then click Try Basic for free under the Basic — $10/month plan.

9

Understand the free trial

CookieYes gives you 14 days completely free before anything is charged. You'll even get an email reminder 3 days before that trial ends, so there are no surprises.

Read over the trial timeline, then click Add payment details to continue. (You will not be charged today.)

10

Enter your payment details

A card is required to hold your spot for after the trial, but as the previous screen showed, today's charge is $0.00.

Fill in your card details (or choose PayPal instead), then confirm. This information goes directly to CookieYes's secure payment provider (Stripe) — it never passes through me.

11

You're on the Basic plan! 🎉

You'll land on a page listing your site with its plan clearly marked. That confirms the upgrade went through.

✅ Part 2 complete — your plan now says "Basic (Trial)." You're ready for the last step.
3

Adding Me as an Administrator

The very last step — one invite, and I can help manage your banner going forward.

12

Open the Team page

This is the settings area where you control who else has access to your account.

Click the small profile icon in the top-right corner of any page, then choose Team from the menu.

13

Invite a new user

This is how you bring a helper into your account without ever sharing your password.

Click + Invite new user.

14

Add my email and set the role to Admin

This tells CookieYes who I am and exactly how much I'm allowed to do — full access to manage your banner, but never your billing or your card.

Choose your organisation from the dropdown, type in the email address below, select Admin under "Role" (not Editor — Editor can't fully manage things), then click Invite user.

15

You're finished! ✅

Your Team page will now show a second row with "Admin" as the role and "Pending" as the status — that's confirmation the invite went out. I'll get an email to accept it, and once I do, my status will switch to "Active."
